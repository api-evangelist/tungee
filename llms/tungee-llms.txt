# Tungee

> Tungee (Guangzhou Tungee Technology Co., Ltd., 探迹科技) is a Chinese AI sales-intelligence company backed by Qiming Venture Partners. Its Tungee AI Sales Cloud covers the full B2B sales cycle -- AI prospecting over a 300M+ company knowledge graph, inbound capture, outreach (call center, SMS, email), and an AI CRM -- plus a Sales GPT agent line, a mobile app, and DingTalk and WeCom editions. The group also operates Futern, its international AI sales agent brand. Tungee markets an "API接口灵活调用" (flexible open data and function-call interface) on its CRM integration page, but publishes no developer portal, no API reference and no machine-readable specification.
