# Tungee

> Tungee (Guangzhou Tungee Technology Co., Ltd., 探迹科技) is a Chinese AI sales-intelligence company backed by Qiming Venture Partners. Its Tungee AI Sales Cloud covers the full B2B sales cycle -- AI prospecting over a 300M+ company knowledge graph, inbound capture, outreach (call center, SMS, email), and an AI CRM -- plus a Sales GPT agent line, a mobile app, and a DingTalk edition. The group also operates Futern, an international B2B leads product. Tungee publishes no public developer portal or API documentation.
